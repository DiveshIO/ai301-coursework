## Rules I write by

<!-- 3-5 rules, drafted from the lecture's slide-12 moment. Each rule

Rule: Keep it clean

use simple and direct language which makes it easier for everyone to understand

- Wrong: "I had conducted a these sequence of steps and obtain this unexpected result which was discovered."
- Right: "I followed the steps above and got X error"

Rule: Use evidence

describe what you oberved instead of talking about other person reproduction

- Wrong: "Same as the other repo"
- Right: "I ran the same command and obtain the same error in my environment"

Rule: Be Specific

explain exactly what we tested versus their claims

- Wrong: "The issues is still broken"
- Right: "I ran X steps and got the same error and explain more about the error"

Rule: Use own evidence

don't claim something occured if you don't have enough evidence

- Wrong: "The definitely fixes the problem"
- Right: "I tested the change and the error seems been fixed"

## Things I never post

<!-- A short list. Promises you cannot keep, tones you refuse,
shortcuts you know you reach for when tired. The skill quotes this

I never claim a fix works without showing proof it works
I never claim someone else's works and call it my own.
I never hide information if I couldn't reproduce the issues
I never claim something I haven't done
I never make promises which I cannot satisify.
