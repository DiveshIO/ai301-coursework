## Environment

<!-- Where the environment record lives, and what a sufficient one
looks like against the issue's stated target. -->

  Lives: *They live in environement section and in the report. In live mode, check repo, the issues, and comments*

  Good Looks *The repo version, runtime, dependencies, any information about the environement which aligns with the issues*

## Steps

<!-- Where the reproduction steps live, and what makes them followable
by a stranger, starting state to trigger. -->

  Lives: *In the reproduction report. In live mode, check the reproduction issues for commands that were used.*

  Good looks  *The steps that are shown show where we started and what we did to obtain the issue. basically easily following the step without being in doubt.*

## Behavior shown

<!-- Where the artifacts live (output excerpts, logs, screenshots),
and what it means for an artifact to show the issue's behavior rather
than an adjacent one. -->
  Lives *in the report's output, logs, screenshots, and GitHub issues.*

  Good Looks *The evidence shows how the issues could not be reproduced and evidence for what the issues cause.*

## Honesty

<!-- Where claims and their backing meet: how to tell a report that
says exactly what happened (including an honest cannot-reproduce) from
one that claims more than its evidence shows. -->

  Lives *in the claim, report, and github comments.*
  
  Good Looks *The report should only say what the evidence shows. If the issue can't be reproduced, say what happened*

## Comms

<!-- Where the words meet the repo: the claim comment against the

  Good Looks *The comments should contain the actual finding and link to the evidence. follow the repo's requirement*
